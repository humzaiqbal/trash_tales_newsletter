Jul 17, 2026

Episode 83

Monday

After another day in the mines I went home and did nothing.

Tuesday

See Monday

Wednesday

On my way to working with GPT 5.5 I ran into “Granola Girlie” who happened to be taking the same bus as me and we chatted a bit about hiking and the wheel on her bike unfortunately getting stolen. Once I finished praising GPT 5.5 for being a good model I went to Midnight Runners with “Hell Yeah”. The run itself was pretty solid although I accidentally paused my Strava during one of my faster miles which was a little bit unfortunate but not terrible.

After the run “Hell Yeah” and I went to In-N-Out and I wolfed down 3 double doubles like a madman.

Thursday

After a day vibing with the code I went to an improv session hosted by “Hell Yeah”. I learned last night that every Thursday he gets some of his friends together and does improv games for an hour which I quite enjoyed. We started with some basic warmup games and then went on to actual scenes. It wasn’t totally freeform wherein you get a word from the audience and do whatever you want, there was some additional structure.

The first game we played had us do two person scenes wherein we get a word from the audience and one person starts the scene as high status and the other as low status with the two switching during the scene. So the person who was once high status is now low status and vice versa. In my scene I started out having a cool truck that the other person wanted only for it to turn out I had drugs in the truck and I had to go with him to mine chocolate in Africa so I don’t get sent to jail and my wife doesn’t find out.

In the next game we played, two people start the scene talking about a third person and then that third person joins. In mine me and another person are planning for a community festival that our friend would join us in. However it turns out our friend likes to take corn on the cob and shove them up his butt and offer them to children. He called it a cobbing too. We tried desperately to get him not to do this with terrible results.

It was quite fun and I thoroughly enjoyed it. It was fun getting back into improv after not doing it for so long.

Later I went to bar night with “The Orchestra,” “The Mosher,” “The Awepartment,” and some of “The Awepartment’s” friends who I don’t have newsletter nicknames for including “M”, “E”, and yeah I don’t remember the name of the last one but she is only visiting for a little while so that’s ok. One of you gets the newsletter. Do you have any thoughts on what I should call you? Maybe Eagle since that’s what your contact picture is. Nah, that sucks. I don't like it. Well I’ll farm you for cool tidbits the next time I see you. I learned sadly that “The Orchestra” will be leaving for N.Y. But on the other hand I’m happy he’s gonna be starting a new role that he’s excited for.

Friday

Once I finished doing not just a bit of work but dare I say a whole byte of it I met up with “The Quant” and we walked around for a while. Astute readers may remember that “The Quant” is currently competing in a step challenge for his company where the winning team gets a prize beyond what any of you can even imagine in the hypothetical scenario where you are all 5 year olds who can’t even imagine what $200 in shoe store giftcards even look like. During this month I can push him to do more and more walks with me and I can hardly say no to an opportunity like this. We start in the Mission and make our way to GGP. On the way we get some boba and “The Quant” gets hit on by some mentally unwell gay dude.

It’s actually kinda funny in a very wtf way. We walk out of the boba store and the guy asks if we bought any for him. I told him no pretty straight up and kept walking. The guy got a bit annoyed with me and started talking with us some more. I don’t fully remember the details but I think he was mentioning how attractive he was or something but then he got interested in “The Quant”. For whatever reason I was being a bit snarky with the guy and he was like “I’m not talking to you … I’m never talking to you”. At one point we were waiting for a walk sign to turn on and he said “Can’t you make it go faster, you work here” and then he shouted across the street “He works here!!” I don’t think there’s really a good response at this point other than extricating from the situation.

Once the walk sign turned we crossed and he started talking to other people so we took our leave of the situation. The rest of the walk was much more straight forward. We go to GGP, “The Quant” gets a drink, and we talk about life. All in all quite solid.

Saturday

The weather was cloudy and cold so I decided not to go to Marina Run Club today. I thought about it for a while but then I was just like … nah. Once I do make it out of the ol bed I go to trash pickup with one of “The Ravenkeeper’s” interns whose name I’m now forgetting and a few other people I know but don’t have newsletter nicknames.One of them works at Clorox doing science there so maybe I’ll call him “Bleaching to the choir” but it’s not an official nickname yet.

After pickup I hopped on a call with “The Professor,” “The Game Master,” “The Sparkle,” “The Plower,” and “The K-pop dancer”. Since I talked with “The Professor” last week during my long walk I already heard most of the updates on his life (tl;dr his first paper with the lab he leads will be coming soon) so instead I reminded folks that while the Odyssey movie coming out is cool and all I think if mainstream Hollywood weren’t cowards they’d do Oedipus instead of Odysseus.

Then I went to Costco to finally get some new glasses. My glasses broke a few months ago and my eyesight is in that interesting spot where it’s good enough that I don’t “need” glasses to get by but it can be hard to read some somewhat further away signs and stuff without them so I procrastinated on getting new ones until today. Sadly I learned they won’t give new glasses if my last eye exam was more than 2 years ago so I signed up for one next week and will have to come back.

On my way out I was walking about and I ran into “The Trivial Pursuit” and he spontaneously invited me to watch the England vs Norway game with him and some of his other friends which was quite a bit of fun. We went to this bar near Union Square called Golden Gate Tap Room which was packed despite its massive size and multiple floors. I’ve grown more into watching soccer over the course of this world cup so I can start to appreciate the game more even when people don’t score as much. Granted I don’t think I’ll watch much after the world cup when general enthusiasm has died down but still. I’m rooting for Norway here because I think they’re slightly the underdog plus all the fervor about that Eric guy. Unfortunately I have to leave around half time to get ready for the dinner party that I’m hosting.

As I finish doing my shopping for the Plov and Pasta I stop by a bar near me just in time to see England get its second goal of the match and sadly beat Norway and move onto the semifinals.

I then go home and cook my Plov and Pasta for the dinner party. THis is my first time trying to cook two dishes at the same time and overall it went well but I do think I undercooked my Plov rice. The flavor was there though especially in the meat which is the most important part so it wasn’t a total loss but it could have been better.

Once everything was ready “Hell Yeah,” “The Taco,” and “The Pickleballer” came over and we hung out, played coup, and talked about life.

Sunday

Today I went on a hike to Alamere falls with “Granola Girlie,” “Civic Minded,” “The Raja of Rhythm,” and “The Yogi”. Alamere falls happens to be up in Marin county which means I drove up through my old stomping grounds.

The hike itself started pretty smoothly. There wasn’t a whole lot of elevation gained or lost at any particular moment so it felt a lot like one big walk.

After about 6 miles of this we made it to a beach where the falls was

The road to the falls itself does get a little bit challenging. We reach a point for example where we are right by the water and to cross we either have to take off our shoes and socks and sprint when the waves have receded, or go along some sketchy slippery rock. Most of us chose the former not wanting to risk the rock except for “Granola Girlie” who managed to do the latter earning her a mini shoutout for her rock maneuverability skills and her ability to have done the hike while keeping her shoes on.

After that we make it to the falls

We take some time to chill by the beach, relax, and enjoy the views before heading back. One piece of the story I didn’t share yet is that when you are hiking to the falls there are two ways to get there. Go the long way down and through the beach which is what we took to get there, or take a short cut. While the short cut saves a couple miles we’re told that it has a bunch of poison oak overgrowth and it’s very steep. We weigh our options for a while until we meet some people who came down from the shortcut who told us it’s pretty manageable but that there is a bunch of overgrowth. Since “Civic Minded” and “The Yogi” were wearing shorts that made it a bit harder but you know what they say, restriction breeds creativity. We were able to fashion some of the jackets as makeshift pants to help protect from the overgrowth.

With that in mind we’re ready to take on the shortcut. It starts off with what was in my opinion a somewhat brutal climb up some very smooth and slippery rock. Longer time readers may remember that I have a bit of a phobia of losing my balance and falling which is part of what made urchin foraging a bit difficult for me and sadly that comes back here a little bit. I manage with some help from “The Raja of Rhythm” to climb one particularly difficult part. After that though the shortcut is pretty manageable and we manage to shave 2-3 miles off our time so it was well worth it.

In the end the hike took us about 11 miles to do

We get back and make our way home when the car hits a bit of a bump in the (off) road and bottomed out. Oil can be smelt and fluid can be seen dripping out of the car with uncertainty plaguing us. Initially we’re all taken aback and trying to figure out what to do. Can we drive the car? Would it breakdown right now and send us to a fiery death as our life flashes before my eyes and I remember that one kid from middle school who was really fuckin weird thinking maybe I should have been nicer to him? Eventually we came to the realization that if we get stuck out here it’s going to be almost impossible for anyone to come help us since we don’t have service here so we drive it to the closest town which happens to be the small town of Bolinas.

Upon arriving “The Yogi” ventures out to try to seek a mechanic while the rest of us stay and do some more car triage. Ace mechanic Claude highly discourages us from going any further thinking this may be a problem with the breaking fluid which would be incredibly dangerous to drive further in. “The Yogi” comes back and tells us there may be a guy named Sean and to find him we have to go to the local bar. This town is small enough that there is only one real bar here to go to, and the first person sitting at the bar does indeed know Sean! Unfortunately Sean is in Richmond right now so we are SOL. Our only option now is to call AAA and try to find some way back home. Unfortunately AAA won’t be able to give all 5 of us a ride in the truck so we have to be creative here. Ideally we could catch an Uber and pay a bunch of $$$ but worst case we may need to call up some friends. We don’t want to involve others but if we get desperate enough that may be our only option.

But before then we still need to deal with AAA to get the car out of here. The cell signal isn’t great in this town so for a while we stay near the bar where we can get wifi somewhat well as long as we are right by the front. After some time in the struggle bus we get a hold of AAA and they tell us it will be about an hour before they can get here. Now how reliable this hour is is a bit of an open question. Is it a Japanese train hour where they will be there on the dot and it’s national news if they are 20 seconds late? A cable guy who may come in 3-4 hours if you’re lucky? Let’s see.

While here may as well make the most of Bolinas right. We walk around, go to some markets, and sit outside and enjoy life. I workshop a new ice breaker question “What’s something you wish more people knew about?”. I realized the old question I was using was “What’s something cool or interesting about you that people wouldn’t expect?” can sometimes put people on the spot if they don't feel they have something cool about themselves to share, whereas the new question lets people talk about things beyond themselves and get into values or other interesting things that people may not share otherwise. Through this question I learned that the U.S has been handicapping Cuba more than I expected through a wide variety of trade embargos (I knew there were some but not to the extent that is happening).

As the hour draws to an end we go back to the car to see if AAA would come. In the UI it says one of the more ominous words imaginable “soon”. “Civic minded” tries calling the driver to no success. After a couple minutes we walk back to the bar to find a spot we can see the truck coming in the distance but then the truck does come! This was huge! Soon was soon! Now things are actually starting to proceed to plan. Once the car is escorted out we now need to think about our own way home.

We furiously call Ubers and Lyfts for a while. We have everyone call one, we have people put different destinations like SF, Mill Valley, etc whatever we think will best increase our chances of getting one. Unfortunately we kept getting hit with “Not available right now”. At this point we wonder if we may need to call a friend after all. Eventually we source some saints who say they may be able to help but we still feel uncomfortable calling unless we *REALLY NEED* it. Eventually one of the rides “The Yogi” calls pans through and it looks like we have our ticket out of here.

Unfortunately though “The Yogi’s” ride cancels on us :(. We go back to calling and at this point I realize we may need to call regular Ubers as well as the XLs we originally were. Yes we can only fit normally in an XL but this is not a normal situation and sometimes you gotta bend the rules a little bit to get what you want.

Eventually a regular Uber says it will come from my calling and will be there in 14 minutes. I’m a bit skeptical so I keep tracking the Uber and it still just says 14 minutes. There is no movement after many minutes and we wonder if the Uber is trying to get me to cancel. At this point I’m making them cancel. I'm not squandering an opportunity like this. Though after a few more minutes it looks like this may not be the move after all. We don’t know what to do just yet so we take a little break and chat about other things. I get a phone call from an unknown number which I answer on reflex only for it to be the driver! He’s here! I sprint out and see him! We did it! We got an Uber! Now for the part where we ask him to technically break the rules and take all of us which he does. While we’re at the bar waiting for the Uber “Civic Minded” is having a conversation with someone based on both being from San Diego which is very wholesome but sadly I must interrupt because I don’t want to keep the driver waiting.

I ordered this Uber from Bolinas to Mill Valley on the thought a driver would be more likely to agree than going all the way back to SF. Our plan from there was either to take the bus or get another Uber. However after some internal deliberation amongst ourselves we ask the driver if he can take us all the way back to SF. At first I was a bit worried he may not like the idea and I didn’t want to jeopardize the ride we have now but he agreed. Originally we strike the idea of paying him directly so Uber doesn’t get money as the middleman (sorry “The Writer” we’re not giving you that much $$), but he is chill with us just changing the destination to SF.

After some more time in the car we eventually make it back to SF. Sadly for whatever reason I started getting a bad stomach ache and could feel the trail mix starting to make it’s way up a little bit. Not all the way mind you just some, uh, “friendly nudges” if you will. I get off a little bit early to make it less cramped and so my stomach has a bit more room to breathe which is sad because I wanted to see the adventure all the way through to the end when the AAA guy makes it back to “Civic Minded’s” house but I think I made it a pretty good chunk of the way. As I walk back home I’m pleased to find that my stomach gets better and I was not going to have to pay a visit to the porcelain throne as I feared. I just needed a bit of air. I then order some celebratory Plov and wolf that shit down grateful to have real food.
